A Dynamic Island
for Windows.

Lumi turns the top of your screen into a living notch. Music, a focus timer, todos, notes, clipboard history, AI quick-ask, weather and more, one glance up.

Download for Windows See what it does

Free · v1.0.0 · 217 KB · Windows 10 (1809+) and 11 · No admin needed

Quiet when you're busy.
There when you glance up.

At rest, the notch shows the one thing that matters right now. When something happens, it opens just long enough for you to see it.

Fifteen little apps,
one notch.

Hover to open. Scroll or swipe sideways to flip between apps. Click the title to see them all.

  • Notes
  • Calendar
  • File shelf
  • Teleprompter
  • Downloads
  • Themes
  • Notch or floating pill
  • Ctrl+Alt+D to hide

Install in ten seconds.

  1. 1
    Download LumiSetup.exe.
  2. 2
    Run it. It installs for you only, no admin needed, and adds Lumi to the Start menu.
  3. 3
    Look up. Lumi appears at the top of your screen. Hover over it.
Smart App Control or SmartScreen warning? Lumi isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may warn about it or, with Smart App Control turned on, block it. Code signing is on the way.

Uninstall any time from Settings > Apps. Silent install: LumiSetup.exe /S.

Stays on your PC.

No account, no tracking

Lumi doesn't collect anything. Notes, todos and settings live in your user folder.

Clipboard in memory only

History disappears when Lumi quits, and anything your password manager marks as secret is skipped.

Only two outside calls

Weather comes from wttr.in. The AI box opens Claude or ChatGPT in your browser, only when you use it.
